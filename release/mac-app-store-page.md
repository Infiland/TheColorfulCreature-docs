**Description:**

The Colorful Creature is a difficult platformer with puzzle color mechanics.

Play over 350 handcrafted levels full of unusual items, blocks and hazards. Change gravity, alter your speed, jump higher and teleport as you work out a route to the finish.

TAKE ON THE CAMPAIGN
Explore colorful worlds, overcome demanding platforming challenges and face the bosses waiting along the way. Learn from each attempt and keep pushing forward.

PUT YOUR SKILLS TO THE TEST
Tackle built-in challenge levels, chase better times and earn medals. Try Endless Run for an ongoing queue of handcrafted levels.

CREATE AND PLAY TOGETHER
Build your own stages in the desktop level editor, and play together in local multiplayer. Mac controllers are supported.

MAKE THE CREATURE YOUR OWN
Unlock skins by completing tasks and collect hats from the hat merchant. Mix them to create your own look.

Created by independent developer Infiland, The Colorful Creature rewards persistence, experimentation and a well-timed jump.
