# The Colorful Creature — iOS/iPadOS App Store draft

Status: copy and screenshots saved in the App Store Connect **1.2.0** draft. Build 2 is attached; no review submission or public release has occurred.

## English (U.S.) listing

**Name:** The Colorful Creature

**Subtitle:** Color puzzles meet platforming (saved in App Store Connect)

**Promotional text:** A tricky platformer full of colorful puzzles. Explore the campaign, tackle built-in challenges, and chase your best run in Endless Run.

**Description:**

The Colorful Creature is a challenging platformer built around color puzzles. Guide a small creature through handcrafted levels filled with changing blocks, hazards, coins, and surprises.

Explore the campaign across five worlds, replay levels to improve your time, and test your skills in built-in challenges. When you want a different pace, jump into Endless Run and see how far you can go.

Unlock skins and hats as you play. On iPhone and iPad, move, jump, and interact with adjustable touch controls.

Optional rewarded ads offer extra in-game rewards. You can play the campaign and challenges without watching them.

**Keywords:** platformer,puzzle,color,challenge,endless,arcade,indie,skins,hats

**What's New in 1.2.0:** The Colorful Creature comes to iPhone and iPad with touch controls, the campaign, built-in challenges, Endless Run, and customization. This release also includes stability and interface improvements.

**Category:** Games, with Action and Puzzle subcategories (saved in App Store Connect).

**Support URL:** https://github.com/Infiland/TheColorfulCreature/issues

**Copyright:** © 2026 Infiland

**Price:** €2.49 in euro storefronts and US$2.49 in the United States, with Apple's equivalent tiers elsewhere. This is the shared iOS/macOS universal-purchase record.

**App Review notes to save after adding review contact details:**

The game works offline and does not require an account. Game Center sign-in is optional for achievements and leaderboards. For review, choose New Game from the main menu for the campaign, Level Select to replay levels, Challenges for built-in challenge stages, or Endless Run for the sequential run mode. The game is landscape-only on iPhone and iPad. Touch controls can be moved and resized in Settings > Controls Config. Rewarded advertising is voluntary; no ad is required to progress.
